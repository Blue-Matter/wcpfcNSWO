4  Performance Metrics

4.1 Management Objectives

The CMPs are evaluated against the candidate management objectives specified in the candidate inputs for the MSE (WCPFC 2026, Attachment 7, Table 1) (Table 4.1):

  1. Safety: Maintain spawning biomass above the limit reference point (LRP; 17% \(SB_0\)) with at least 80% probability.

  2. Status: Maintain fishing mortality at or below the target reference point with at least 50% probability. The candidate target reference points are F\(_{40\%}\), F\(_{35\%}\), F\(_{30\%}\), the F targets of the HCRs (Section 5.2). Status is evaluated against the F target of the HCR of each CMP, and is also reported against each of the candidate target reference points. Following the advice of the ISC that the Status indicator should incorporate biomass as well as fishing mortality (WCPFC 2026, para. 46), the probability that spawning biomass is above each of the candidate threshold reference points (35%, 30%, 25% \(SB_0\)) is also reported with the Status metrics.

  3. Stability: Changes in the TAC between management periods should be relatively gradual.

  4. Yield: Maintain catches above average historical catch levels.

The CMPs are tuned in the Base Case OM to maximise the mean annual catch subject to the Safety objective (probability of at least 0.8) and the Status objective (probability of at least 0.5 against the F target of the HCR of the CMP; Section 5.6). The Stability and Yield metrics are not used in the tuning; the trade-off between TAC stability and catch is shown by the two TAC change limits of each CMP.

4.2 Primary Performance Metrics

All performance metrics are calculated over the 30 years in which the CMPs set the TAC (2028–2057), for each OM and CMP, from the true spawning biomass, fishing mortality, and catch in the OM (not the CMPs’ estimates). Spawning biomass and fishing mortality are averaged over the quarters of each calendar year, and catch is summed. The following notation is used:

  • \(s = 1, \ldots, S\) indexes the simulations and \(y \in \mathcal{Y}\) the \(N_Y\) = 30 years;
  • \(SB_{s,y}\) is the annual female spawning biomass, \(SB_0\) the equilibrium unfished spawning biomass, and \(SB_{\text{MSY}}\) the spawning biomass at MSY;
  • \(F_{s,y}\) is the annual apical fishing mortality;
  • \(C_{s,y}\) is the annual catch (total removals of all fleets, in t);
  • \(\text{TAC}_{s,c}\) is the TAC set in management cycle \(c = 1, \ldots, N_C\), and \(\Delta_{s,c} = \lvert \text{TAC}_{s,c} - \text{TAC}_{s,c-1} \rvert / \text{TAC}_{s,c-1}\) is the relative change in the TAC;
  • \(\mathrm{I}(\cdot)\) is 1 if the condition is true and 0 otherwise.

Safety: the probability that spawning biomass is above the LRP in any given year:

\[ P_{\text{Safety}} = \frac{1}{S N_Y} \sum_{s} \sum_{y} \mathrm{I}\left(SB_{s,y}/SB_0 > 0.17\right) \]

Status: the probability that fishing mortality is at or below the F target, and the median ratio of the F target to fishing mortality:

\[ P_{\text{Status}} = \frac{1}{S N_Y} \sum_{s} \sum_{y} \mathrm{I}\left(F_{s,y} \leq F_{\text{target}}\right) \]

\[ \frac{F_{\text{target}}}{F} = \frac{1}{S} \sum_{s} \operatorname*{median}_{y} \left(\frac{F_{\text{target}}}{F_{s,y}}\right) \]

where \(F_{\text{target}}\) is the F target of the HCR of the CMP (F\(_{40\%}\), F\(_{35\%}\), F\(_{30\%}\)), the apical fishing mortality that reduces the female spawning potential ratio to the target level. \(F_{\text{target}}\) is calculated for each OM and simulation, relative to \(F_{\text{MSY}}\), in the last year with both reference points, and the annual \(F/F_{\text{target}}\) is calculated from the annual \(F/F_{\text{MSY}}\).

The probability that spawning biomass is above a threshold reference point (ThRP) is also reported with the Status metrics, for each of the candidate ThRPs (35%, 30%, 25% \(SB_0\)):

\[ P_{\text{SB > ThRP}} = \frac{1}{S N_Y} \sum_{s} \sum_{y} \mathrm{I}\left(SB_{s,y}/SB_0 > \text{ThRP}\right) \]

Like the other metrics, this is calculated from the true spawning biomass in the OM. The same ThRPs are used for every CMP, so the metric does not depend on the ThRP of the HCR of the CMP, and CMPs with different HCRs can be compared against the same thresholds.

Stability: the probability that the TAC changes by no more than 10% between management cycles, and the probability of no management change, i.e. that the TAC changes by no more than 1% (the CMPs leave the TAC unchanged when the change would be less than 1%):

\[ P_{\text{Stability}} = \frac{1}{S N_C} \sum_{s} \sum_{c} \mathrm{I}\left(\Delta_{s,c} \leq 0.1\right) \]

\[ P_{\text{No change}} = \frac{1}{S N_C} \sum_{s} \sum_{c} \mathrm{I}\left(\Delta_{s,c} \leq 0.01\right) \]

Because the TAC change limit of the CMPs is either 10% or larger, \(P_{\text{Stability}}\) is 1 for the CMPs with a 10% limit.

Yield: the probability that the annual catch is at least the mean historical catch in the reference years (\(\bar{C}_{\text{ref}}\), from the OM), for two reference periods (2008–2010 and 2016–2021), and the mean annual catch:

\[ P_{\text{Yield}} = \frac{1}{S N_Y} \sum_{s} \sum_{y} \mathrm{I}\left(C_{s,y} \geq \bar{C}_{\text{ref}}\right) \]

\[ \bar{C} = \frac{1}{S N_Y} \sum_{s} \sum_{y} C_{s,y} \]

4.3 Secondary Performance Metrics

Status against each candidate target reference point: \(P_{\text{Status}}\) and \(F_{\text{target}}/F\) are also calculated for each of F\(_{40\%}\), F\(_{35\%}\), F\(_{30\%}\), for every CMP, so CMPs with different HCRs can be compared against the same target.

Probability that SB is above the ThRP of the HCR: \(P_{\text{SB > ThRP}}\) calculated with the ThRP of the HCR of each CMP, i.e. the proportion of years in which the true stock status is above the point where the HCR starts to reduce fishing (Section 5.2).

Probability of no breach of the LRP: the probability that spawning biomass remains above the LRP in every year; a simulation in which spawning biomass falls below the LRP in any year is a breach:

\[ P_{\text{No breach}} = \frac{1}{S} \sum_{s} \mathrm{I}\left(\min_{y \in \mathcal{Y}} SB_{s,y}/SB_0 > 0.17\right) \]

Average TAC variability: the mean absolute relative change in the TAC between management cycles:

\[ \text{AAVY} = \frac{1}{S N_C} \sum_{s} \sum_{c} \Delta_{s,c} \]

Kobe green quadrant: the probability that the stock is not overfished and overfishing is not occurring:

\[ P_{\text{Kobe}} = \frac{1}{S N_Y} \sum_{s} \sum_{y} \mathrm{I}\left(\frac{SB_{s,y}}{SB_{\text{MSY}}} > 1 \ \text{and} \ \frac{F_{s,y}}{F_{\text{MSY}}} < 1\right) \]

Stock status: the mean \(SB/SB_0\), the lowest annual \(SB/SB_0\), and the mean \(SB/SB_{\text{MSY}}\) and \(F/F_{\text{MSY}}\).

Relative yield: the mean annual catch relative to the CMP with the highest mean annual catch in the same OM.

4.4 Time Windows

Safety, mean catch, and mean \(SB/SB_0\) are also calculated for three time windows:

  1. Short: the first 10 years (2028–2037);
  2. Medium: the years between the short and long windows (2038–2047);
  3. Long: the last 10 years (2048–2057).

The metrics calculated over all 30 years can hide differences in when the outcomes occur. The windows show how the outcomes change over the projection period:

  • Safety shows when the risk to the stock occurs. For example, a CMP can meet the Safety objective over the whole period while most of the years below the LRP occur early or late in the period.
  • Mean catch shows how the catch is distributed over time. For example, it distinguishes CMPs with high initial catches that decline later from CMPs with lower initial catches that increase.
  • Mean \(SB/SB_0\) shows the trajectory of the stock, i.e., whether it is rebuilding, stable, or declining under each CMP.
