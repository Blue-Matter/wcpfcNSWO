# wcpfcNSWO: North Pacific Swordfish Management Strategy Evaluation

Operating models, management procedures, and analysis tools for the
Management Strategy Evaluation (MSE) of North Pacific swordfish,
developed for the Western and Central Pacific Fisheries Commission
(WCPFC).
